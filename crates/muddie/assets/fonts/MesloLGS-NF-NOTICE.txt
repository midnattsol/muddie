MesloLGS NF Regular

Meslo LG is a customised version of Apple's Menlo typeface by André Berg,
itself derived from Bitstream Vera Sans Mono. MesloLGS NF is Meslo LG S
patched with additional glyphs by Roman Perepelitsa, from
https://github.com/romkatv/powerlevel10k-media
